# Grounding and useful reuse on PR15

Three natural-track campaigns used the same worker (Luna 6 low), the same two returning Sol 6.1 medium reviewers, PR15 at `628a6a1`, main at `173acc9`, and evaluator contract /17. Each campaign had 14 matched tasks per arm (four learning and ten reuse) and fresh stores per workload and arm. This is a descriptive analysis of 126 completed sessions on 14 distinct tasks. The comparisons use the frozen grades. All worker, quality and final manifests checked against their hashes.

| Arm (42 sessions each) | Learning gross | Reuse gross | Total gross | Grounding passed | Strict quality passed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PR15 | 633,815 | 1,435,794 | 2,069,609 | 25 | 25 |
| Main | 669,085 | 1,493,513 | 2,162,598 | 27 | 27 |

## What saved tokens

* Against main, gross savings were 92,989 (4.30%). Counted input fell by 93,701, including 73,856 cached input tokens. Output **increased** by 712. Against No-Colony, savings were 142,141 (6.43%): input fell by 141,149 and output by 992. No-Colony still used less uncached input plus output (408,486 versus 496,233). Gross is the project measure and includes cached input.
* PR15, main and No-Colony completed 99, 113 and 160 MCP calls. Both Colony arms batched: PR15 made 31 batches and main made 33. PR15 made **10 versus 18** memory lookups and **5 versus 13** fixture searches, with two more source-read calls than main. Against No-Colony, 47 PR15 read calls (including batches) compare with 104 single fixture reads.
* Within each run, pair the same workload and task. Against main, PR15 saved 203,045 tokens across the 14 pairs where it made fewer MCP calls. It spent an extra 42,570 across the 22 equal-call pairs and 67,486 across the six more-call pairs. Against No-Colony, fewer-call pairs saved 228,005 and the remaining pairs cost an extra 85,864. Fewer interactions are consistent with less repeated input. Native usage exposes session totals, so this does not price a specific call or isolate the empty-store hint from worker routing and sample variation. Optional dependency tracing was disabled throughout.
* PR15 was cheaper on 15/42 pairs against main and 18/42 against No-Colony. Against main the worker workload contributed 71,781 of the 92,989 net savings. Against No-Colony the reuse phase saved 175,965 while learning cost an extra 33,824. The aggregate benefit concentrates in particular paths through the tasks.

## Why grounding fell short

PR15's grounding passes were 11, 6 and 8 versus main's 9, 9 and 9. Across all three runs there were 18 matched pairs where both passed, eight where neither passed, nine main-only passes and seven PR15-only passes. The pooled difference is small and several failure modes also affected controls. The two workloads and reused reviewers limit generalization. Of PR15's 17 nonpasses, 14 were failures and three unresolved.

* **A definition was treated as a binding.** Both gateway and worker accept injected dependencies. Reading a concrete retry implementation establishes what that implementation would do if selected. It does not establish the deployed binding. Gateway useful-hit tasks failed grounding in all three runs. Reviewers also identified missing caller evidence tying resolved settings to generic retry parameters.
* **A bound was treated as the executed path.** Two attempts allow one additional retry. On the always-raising worker path, the inner loop raises on its final attempt and the outer loop does not catch it. Multiplying the two configured bounds, or claiming two actual outer calls, contradicted the observed code. Some answers added concrete wire counts beyond the requested outer behavior.
* **A limited search was treated as evidence of absence.** Three PR15 miss-task failures had only README content or no observed file contents to support a broader repository claim. A literal miss cannot rule out a derived value. Direct-file value tasks passed grounding in all six PR15 sessions; making all tasks read more would poorly target the problem.
* **One procedural detail was unverifiable by the reviewers.** The third run's gateway publisher reported a specific ID. Its raw receipt contained that ID, while the sanitized review packet confirmed storage and omitted the ID. Grounding remained unresolved. This is a receipt-visibility issue, distinct from a false source claim. The other two unresolved cases concerned missing caller coverage. Existing adapter instructions already mention scope and injected dependencies; repeating the whole policy adds cost without supplying missing evidence.

## Why the verified-reuse count is zero

For the headline metric the report requires a reuse-phase consumer, a verified hit from a successful prior publisher, a successful consumer, zero additional research attempts, and no unapproved calls. See `scripts/token_evaluation/evidence.py` and `report.py`. A task named useful_hit is an opportunity, not an observed hit.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worker cold learning stored a behavior finding. The same-snapshot useful-hit lookup returned `no_relevant_trail`. A later reuse lookup matched the stored ID on changed sources, returned stale, withheld the saved text, and delivered no current reads. | Four research units were used in the stale session. No valid reuse-phase hit. |
| 2 | No publication attempt on either Colony arm. PR15 issued no reuse-phase lookups. | The fresh stores had no learned finding available. |
| 3 | Gateway cold learning stored a behavior finding. The next learning task retrieved the same ID with validated source evidence and completed with zero repository research. The later same-snapshot useful-hit lookup returned `no_relevant_trail`. | The learning consumer passed the evidence-level useful-reuse check. Its phase and the publisher's unresolved grade independently excluded it from the headline count. |

Main also stored two findings and had zero raw hits. On PR15, four of six workload-stores remained empty: two of six substantive cold-learning sessions published, and the six partial-learning questions asked for isolated default values. Publication was conditional and non-attempts have no recorded motive. Across 30 PR15 reuse sessions there were six lookup calls, five misses and the one stale match. For the two stores with successful writes, **both corresponding useful-hit questions actually performed lookup**. More reminders to look up cannot fix those misses. Their questions use references such as “the same production setup” or “the same priority job setup.” The saved behavior prose describes precedence and termination, but omits the concrete resolved values these follow-ups ask for. Those are hypotheses about matching and sufficiency, not evidence of a particular rejected gate. The public miss receipt does not distinguish them.

## Proposed work on a child of PR15

1. **Improve the evidence used for a claim while keeping the cheap path.** In the task adapter, replace existing generic routing text within its current size with guidance tied to the requested claim. For call-chain questions, batch the configuration, caller and operation when paths are known. Include a concrete implementation only when its behavior matters and qualify the result if binding is unknown. Distinguish configured attempts from actual calls on return versus raise. For an absence claim, use remaining research to cover the relevant scope and state any gaps that cannot be resolved. Preserve all requested deliverables and exact continuation offsets. Regression cases should cover an injected interface with two possible implementations, a terminal exception, a computed value missed by literal search, a missing caller and an ordinary direct value. Check instructions/catalog byte size against the parent and ensure a simple task adds no calls. Deterministic tests establish evidence delivery and routing contracts; they cannot establish better model judgments.
2. **Diagnose both missed same-snapshot matches before changing retrieval.** Use existing local retrieval diagnostics with independent, provider-free regression fixtures mirroring the two patterns. Hold the stored question, scoped finding, source hashes and snapshot fixed. Inspect candidate admission, question similarity, procedure and rerank filters, evidence fallback, and whether packing can deliver sufficient current lines. Include an irrelevant sibling query, changed source and conflicting configuration as negative controls. If selection is the failure, make the narrowest change to the identified stage, using trusted same-task context where available. If a match is admitted but incomplete, return the minimal verified caller/configuration/termination evidence and accurately mark remaining gaps. Keep the focused question binding, thresholds for unrelated queries, stale withholding, per-entry quota and response budget. Do not use benchmark IDs, hard-code paraphrases, invent an antecedent or automatically retry. Ship a retrieval change only if the positive and negative fixtures distinguish it from the parent.
3. **Increase the usefulness of eligible writes without requiring publication.** On substantive research tasks, test a replacement for the existing one-time publication cue that asks for already-observed applicability, resolved behavior and its deciding conditions when reusable. Retain all supporting span references or narrow the claim. Preserve read-only mode, explicit remember intent for bare values, secret handling, one attempt and nonblocking answers. If high-quality writes are still not initiated, measure the host's ability to record a best-effort completion decision as a separate change. The MCP receipt alone cannot tell us whether the worker deliberately abstained. For requested publication-status details, keep them answerable; consider a separate evaluator change to bind an exact returned identifier or verify identity without exposing it. Preserve historical grades.
4. **Acceptance and cost accounting.** First run local regressions on each change, including a validated same-snapshot reuse with no extra read, a required follow-up read on partial evidence, rejection of changed/foreign references, and byte-accurate response caps. Retain ordinary batch/quota protections. Keep diagnostics off the model-visible path and avoid extra tool turns to report status. Only in a later authorized native comparison, measure matched PR15-parent and main results on the same tasks, gross tokens for the whole horizon, grounding/strict quality, call counts, and per-stage reuse outcomes. Compare against the three-run distribution and matched tasks, not just the pooled mean. A lower cost cannot offset worse grounding. Keep the existing strict reuse metric; record learning-phase hits and assisted answers separately to identify which stage improved.
